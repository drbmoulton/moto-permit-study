# Numeric and statistical facts: coverage audit

An independent check of whether the question bank (content/m1–m10.json, 249 questions) tests every number or statistic in the 2015 RMV Motorcycle Manual that a test writer could ask about. Checked 2026-10-21 against the manual's text and an image of every page, including the cover, the fee table and the penalty tables on pp.61–64.

**What counts as a fact.** Anything stated with a number, fraction, percentage, angle, distance, time or frequency, age, speed, fee, fine, BAC or suspension length, plus counts and statistical claims in words ("two abreast", "most", "twice as", "more likely than", "leading cause"). Pure cause-and-effect statements with no quantity ("the faster you ride, the more distance you need") are left out, as are sign shapes (p.42) and line types (pp.45–47), for which the manual gives no numbers (m8-19, m8-20 and m8-22 to m8-25 test them).

**Tested by** names a question only when the fact is what it asks or its keyed answer. A fact that appears only in a stem, a distractor or an explanation is marked "—", and the note says where it appears.

**Notes on untested facts** start with a verdict: *Plausible* (a test writer could reasonably ask it), *Unlikely* (vague, trivial, a list count, or off-topic for a Class M test), or *No* (a phone number, form name, citation, sample sign, or contact detail).

**Totals:** 198 facts; 134 tested; 64 untested (24 plausible, 26 unlikely, 14 not testable).

**Plausible but untested on purpose.** Junior-operator rules beyond the three questions the bank keeps for them (m10-09, m10-28, m10-29), since the learner is an adult; moped and scooter fees and rules beyond the speed caps and the moped fine; two figures that are out of date (the p.9 insurance minimums and the p.10 "J" restriction letter); facts every reader meets in a question stem (the p.62 suspension triggers, the under-21 zero-tolerance age); and a few that appear in explanations. decisions.md has the reasoning.

**Note on p.9 insurance minimums.** For policies written or renewed on or after July 1, 2025, Massachusetts raised the compulsory minimums to $25,000/$50,000 bodily injury and $30,000 property damage ([mass.gov notice](https://www.mass.gov/doc/2025-a-new-motor-vehicle-mandatory-coverage-limits/download)). The manual still gives $20,000/$40,000 and $5,000, so a question on this would need the app's caution note.
